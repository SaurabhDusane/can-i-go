# Can I Go?

**College is more than classes. Finish every assignment a day early, and the answer to "can I go?" is yes.**

A [Cubbie](https://hackathon.cubbie.ai) Skill for students. Built for the Cubbie Hackathon, **Track 1**.

## The problem

Students skip the game, the trip and the night out because the work always seems to run late. And most academic problems start the night before the deadline: no time to understand the material, no time to ask for help, and a strong pull to let AI do the assignment. Reminders don't fix either one. They tell you something is due, then leave you alone with it.

Can I Go? fixes both with one rule: **everything is done a day before it's due.** Cubbie plans the hours, follows up until the work is done, balances busy and quiet weeks, and protects the things you want to do.

## What it does

**A finish-by date for every assignment.** One day before each Canvas deadline. Every plan aims there, not at the deadline.

**Ranks work by slack, not just due date.** Free hours before finish-by, minus hours still needed. A 6-hour project due Friday can come before a 1-hour quiz due Wednesday.

**Never fakes precision.** Uses the time an assignment states, or asks you once and remembers. After each assignment it asks how long it really took and learns your pace per course.

**Follows up, every time.** A short reminder before each work block, a "did it happen?" after it, a heads-up the day before finish-by, and a Canvas check on finish-by day. Miss a block and it offers the next free slot. Miss two and it asks if the plan should change.

**Checks Canvas every day.** New assignments get flagged with a first step. Submissions are spotted automatically ("Done a day early."), so you never report anything. When a grade posts, you hear about it: a good job for an A, and help planning the next one when it's lower. Moved due dates get replanned.

**Answers "can I go?"** Send an invite or ask about a plan. It counts the event as busy time and answers **Yes**, **Yes, if** (with the exact blocks), or **Tight** (with options). It never tells you not to go.

**A weekly forecast.** Every Sunday: "Mon ☀️ Tue ⛅ Wed ⛈️ ..." Storm weeks put work first, keep one evening free, and add a short break reminder on heavy days. Chill weeks balance work and fun, and pull a bit of next week's work forward if a storm is coming. After a storm week, the next one is kept lighter.

**Knows your shifts.** Send a photo of your work schedule, and your shifts become busy time.

## What it looks like

> **Cubbie (9 am):** New: CSE 310 project, due Fri Nov 7. About 10 hours for you? Aiming to finish Thu.
>
> **You:** more like 8
>
> **Cubbie:** Got it. 2 hours today 3 to 5 pm, then Mon, Tue, Wed. Lock it in?
>
> **You:** yes
>
> **Cubbie (5:05 pm):** Did the CSE block happen? yes / partly / no
>
> **You:** partly, 1 hr
>
> **Cubbie:** Added an hour Sun 2 pm. Still on track for Thu.
>
> **Cubbie (Thu):** CSE project submitted. Done a day early.

## Built to be reliable

- Checks Canvas before every follow-up, so it never nags you about work you already submitted.
- At most 3 unprompted messages a day, and nothing between 11 pm and 8 am.
- Nothing goes on your calendar until you say yes.
- Goes quiet if you do, and stops everything when you say stop.
- Helps with time, never answers.
